Student Portal - Green University App

Our app makes university life easier by giving you direct access to official Green University links. It’s secure and doesn’t collect your data. The app combines all important links—like for the library, notices, and seating plans—into one easy-to-use place, so you don’t have to remember them all.

Since the app is web-based. we use html and css only. Github link: https://github.com/arifbillah360/GreenUniversity If you have any feedback or questions, please email us at contact@arifbillah.com.
