How to Pre-Register for a Course at Green University of Bangladesh (GUB)

Follow these steps to pre-register for a course at GUB:

1. Go to the Login Portal

Visit the Green University Student Portal.

2. Login

Enter your student credentials (username and password) to access your account.

3. Navigate to Pre-Registration

Once logged in, look for the "Pre-Registration" option in the menu.

4. Select Course

Click on the "Pre-Registration" option and choose the courses you wish to register for.

5. Confirm Registration

Follow any additional prompts to finalize your course selection.
